How the model decides.

The ranker isn't a chatbot. It's a small ensemble that scores each candidate intervention (hydration, sleep, supplement, training swap, meal swap) against three axes:

  • Evidence weightRCT > cohort > case
  • Personal deltadistance from clinical target
  • Adherence costfriction × habit history

Top suggestion surfaces as today's nudge. Anything URGENT bypasses the queue and always shows first.

Why editorial dark.

Most wellness apps look like neon dashboards. We wanted something closer to a notebook a clinician could hand back to a patient — quiet surfaces, one accent colour, tabular numerals, no animation noise. The lime is reserved for adherence and confirmation.
